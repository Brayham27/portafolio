Meet me 🖖

I design for the space where three things have to agree: what people actually need, what the business is trying to achieve, and what the team can realistically build. Most of my work happens in that negotiation.

What I care about most is the part before the pixels, framing the right problem, mapping the system, and getting stakeholders to a shared decision. I've spent the last 10 years inside corporate environments where design has to survive contact with legacy systems, compliance and competing priorities. That's taught me more than any framework.
